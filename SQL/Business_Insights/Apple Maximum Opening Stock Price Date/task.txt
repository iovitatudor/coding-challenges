========================================================================
TASK NAME: Apple Maximum Opening Stock Price Date
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the specific calendar date (or dates) when Apple's opening stock price reached its absolute historical maximum value.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Peak Isolation: Identify the maximum value within the stock opening price column ('open').
* Tie Handling: Structure the filter to return all calendar dates that match this maximum value in case of a pricing tie.
* Columns Needed: date

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: aapl_historical_stock_price
* Core Columns:
  - date (Date) — The specific market trading date.
  - open (Double Precision) — Apple's opening stock price for that day.
