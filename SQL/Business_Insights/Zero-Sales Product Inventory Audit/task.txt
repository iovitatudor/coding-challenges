========================================================================
TASK NAME: Zero-Sales Product Inventory Audit
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify and extract a comprehensive list of all product SKUs that have recorded zero transaction volume or sales history. Output the unique SKU ID and associated market name for these specific products.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table Relationship: Perform a LEFT JOIN from the product dimension table to the customer sales transaction table. This guarantees unsold products remain visible in the query pool.
* Isolation Filter: Isolate records where the foreign key connection in the transaction ledger is completely missing (IS NULL).
* Columns Needed: prod_sku_id, market_name

TABLE SCHEMA:

1. dim_product (dp)
   - prod_sku_id (Text) — Unique SKU identifier for the product.
   - market_name (Text) — The geographic or regional market name.

2. fct_customer_sales (fcs)
   - prod_sku_id (Text) — Foreign key tracking sold product units.
