TASK NAME: Seasonal Sales Analysis (LeetCode 3564)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the most popular product category for each season.
Seasons are defined as:
  - Winter: December, January, February
  - Spring: March, April, May
  - Summer: June, July, August
  - Fall:   September, October, November

Ranking criteria per season:
1. Highest total_quantity sold
2. Highest total_revenue (quantity * price)
3. Lexicographically smaller category name

Return columns: season, category, total_quantity, total_revenue
Order output by season ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: sales
  - sale_id (int)
  - product_id (int)
  - sale_date (date)
  - quantity (int)
  - price (decimal)
  Primary Key: sale_id

* Table: products
  - product_id (int)
  - product_name (varchar)
  - category (varchar)
  Primary Key: product_id
