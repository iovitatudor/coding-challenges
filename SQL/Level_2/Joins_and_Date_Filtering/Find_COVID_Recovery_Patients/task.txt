TASK NAME: Find COVID Recovery Patients (LeetCode 3586)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find patients who tested positive for COVID and later tested negative:
1. Calculate recovery_time in days = (first_negative_date_after_positive - first_positive_date).
2. Only include patients with a valid recovery timeline (both positive and a subsequent negative test).
3. Ignore tests before the first positive or intermediate inconclusive tests.
4. Order output by recovery_time ASC, then patient_name ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: patients
  - patient_id (int)
  - patient_name (varchar)
  - age (int)
  Primary Key: patient_id

* Table: covid_tests
  - test_id (int)
  - patient_id (int)
  - test_date (date)
  - result (varchar) -- 'Positive', 'Negative', 'Inconclusive'
  Primary Key: test_id
