========================================================================
TASK NAME: Employee and Manager Salaries
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find employees who are earning more than their managers. Output the employee's first name along with the corresponding salary.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: employee
* Columns:
  - address (text)
  - age (bigint)
  - bonus (bigint)
  - city (text)
  - department (text)
  - email (text)
  - employee_title (text)
  - first_name (text)
  - id (bigint)
  - last_name (text)
  - manager_id (bigint)
  - salary (bigint)
  - sex (text)
  - target (bigint)
