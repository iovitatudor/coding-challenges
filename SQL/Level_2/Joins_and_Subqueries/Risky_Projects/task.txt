========================================================================
TASK NAME: Risky Projects
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify projects that are overbudget. A project is overbudget if the
total prorated cost of all assigned employees exceeds the project's budget.

Prorate each employee's annual salary based on the exact period they work
on the project relative to a 365-day year:
  prorated_expense = (end_date - start_date) * (annual_salary / 365.0)

Round the total prorated employee expenses UP to the nearest dollar (CEIL).

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Tables:
  - linkedin_projects (id, title, budget, start_date, end_date)
  - linkedin_emp_projects (emp_id, project_id)
  - linkedin_employees (id, first_name, last_name, salary)
