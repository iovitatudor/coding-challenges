TASK NAME: Find Product Recommendation Pairs (LeetCode 3521)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify distinct product pairs frequently purchased together by the same customers:
1. Ensure product1_id < product2_id to prevent duplicates/mirror pairs.
2. Determine how many unique customers purchased both products in a pair.
3. Filter pairs where at least 3 distinct customers purchased both products.
4. Retrieve product categories for both products from ProductInfo.
5. Order output by customer_count DESC, product1_id ASC, product2_id ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: ProductPurchases
  - user_id (int)
  - product_id (int)
  - quantity (int)
  Primary Key: (user_id, product_id)

* Table: ProductInfo
  - product_id (int)
  - category (varchar)
  - price (decimal)
  Primary Key: product_id
