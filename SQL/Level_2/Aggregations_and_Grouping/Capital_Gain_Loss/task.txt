TASK NAME: Capital Gain/Loss
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report the total Capital gain/loss for each stock after buying and
selling the stock one or many times.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Stocks
  - stock_name (varchar)
  - operation (enum: 'Buy', 'Sell')
  - operation_day (int)
  - price (int)
