TASK NAME: Find Drivers with Improved Fuel Efficiency (LeetCode 3601)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find drivers whose fuel efficiency (distance_km / fuel_consumed) improved between H1 and H2:
1. H1 (First half): January to June (Months 1–6)
2. H2 (Second half): July to December (Months 7–12)
3. Calculate average fuel efficiency per trip for each half.
4. Only include drivers who have trips in BOTH halves.
5. Filter for drivers with an efficiency improvement (second_half_avg > first_half_avg).
6. Calculate efficiency_improvement = ROUND(second_half_avg - first_half_avg, 2).
7. Round all average columns to 2 decimal places.
8. Order output by efficiency_improvement DESC, then driver_name ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: drivers
  - driver_id (int)
  - driver_name (varchar)
  Primary Key: driver_id

* Table: trips
  - trip_id (int)
  - driver_id (int)
  - trip_date (date)
  - distance_km (decimal)
  - fuel_consumed (decimal)
  Primary Key: trip_id
