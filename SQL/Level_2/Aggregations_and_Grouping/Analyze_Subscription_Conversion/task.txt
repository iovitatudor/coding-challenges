========================================================================
TASK NAME: Analyze Subscription Conversion
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find users who converted from free trial to paid subscription and calculate:
1. trial_avg_duration: Average daily activity duration during 'free_trial' (rounded to 2 decimals)
2. paid_avg_duration: Average daily activity duration during 'paid' period (rounded to 2 decimals)

Only include users who have records for both 'free_trial' and 'paid'.
Order the output by user_id ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: UserActivity
  - user_id (int)
  - activity_date (date)
  - activity_type (varchar)
  - activity_duration (int)
