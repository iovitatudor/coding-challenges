TASK NAME: Find Overbooked Employees (LeetCode 3611)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find employees who spent more than 50% of their working time (40 hrs/week) in meetings:
1. Group meetings by employee_id and ISO week/year (or week start date).
2. Calculate total meeting duration per employee per week.
3. Mark a week as "meeting-heavy" if total duration > 20 hours.
4. Count meeting-heavy weeks per employee.
5. Filter for employees with at least 2 meeting-heavy weeks (>= 2).
6. Order output by meeting_heavy_weeks DESC, then employee_name ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - employee_name (varchar)
  - department (varchar)
  Primary Key: employee_id

* Table: meetings
  - meeting_id (int)
  - employee_id (int)
  - meeting_date (date)
  - meeting_type (varchar)
  - duration_hours (decimal)
  Primary Key: meeting_id
