========================================================================
TASK NAME: Odd and Even Transactions
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the sum of amounts for odd and even transactions for each day. If there
are no odd or even transactions for a specific date, display 0.
Return the result ordered by transaction_date in ascending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: transactions
  - transaction_id (int)
  - amount (int)
  - transaction_date (date)
