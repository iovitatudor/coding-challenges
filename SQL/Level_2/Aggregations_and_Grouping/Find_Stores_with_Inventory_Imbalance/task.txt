TASK NAME: Find Stores with Inventory Imbalance (LeetCode 3626)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find stores where the most expensive product has a lower stock quantity than the cheapest product:
1. Identify the most expensive product (highest price) and its quantity per store.
2. Identify the cheapest product (lowest price) and its quantity per store.
3. Filter stores that have at least 3 distinct products (COUNT(product_name) >= 3).
4. Filter stores where most_expensive_quantity < cheapest_quantity.
5. Calculate imbalance_ratio = ROUND(cheapest_quantity / most_expensive_quantity::numeric, 2).
6. Order output by imbalance_ratio DESC, then store_name ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: stores
  - store_id (int)
  - store_name (varchar)
  - location (varchar)
  Primary Key: store_id

* Table: inventory
  - inventory_id (int)
  - store_id (int)
  - product_name (varchar)
  - quantity (int)
  - price (decimal)
  Primary Key: inventory_id
