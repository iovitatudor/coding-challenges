========================================================================
TASK NAME: Find Books with No Available Copies
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find all books that are currently borrowed (return_date IS NULL) where the count
of unreturned copies equals total_copies (0 available copies remaining).
Return the result ordered by current_borrowers DESC, then title ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: library_books
  - book_id (int)
  - title (varchar)
  - author (varchar)
  - genre (varchar)
  - publication_year (int)
  - total_copies (int)
* Table: borrowing_records
  - record_id (int)
  - book_id (int)
  - borrower_name (varchar)
  - borrow_date (date)
  - return_date (date)
