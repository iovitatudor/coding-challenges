TASK NAME: Find Consistently Improving Employees (LeetCode 3580)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find employees who have consistently improved their performance over their last three reviews:
1. Must have at least 3 reviews in total.
2. Filter for the most recent 3 reviews per employee based on review_date.
3. Check that the ratings are strictly increasing across these last 3 reviews (r1 < r2 < r3).
4. Calculate improvement_score = (latest_rating - earliest_rating_of_last_3).
5. Order output by improvement_score DESC, then name ASC.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - name (varchar)
  Primary Key: employee_id

* Table: performance_reviews
  - review_id (int)
  - employee_id (int)
  - review_date (date)
  - rating (int)
  Primary Key: review_id
