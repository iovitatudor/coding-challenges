========================================================================
TASK NAME: Find Students Who Improved
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find students who have taken exams in the same subject on at least two
different dates and whose latest score in that subject is strictly higher
than their first score.
Return the result ordered by student_id and subject in ascending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Scores
  - student_id (int)
  - subject (varchar)
  - score (int)
  - exam_date (varchar)

========================================================================
