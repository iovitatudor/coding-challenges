========================================================================
TASK NAME: Restaurant Growth
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Compute the moving average of how much customers paid in a 7-day window
(current day + 6 days before), starting from the first date with 7 full days of data.
Round average_amount to 2 decimal places.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Customer
  - customer_id (int)
  - name (varchar)
  - visited_on (date)
  - amount (int)
