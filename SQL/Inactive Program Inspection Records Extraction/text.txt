TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify and extract all health and safety inspection records that are associated with a business or facility program that is currently flagged as inactive.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Filter Criterion: Filter the rows where the program's lifecycle status is officially recorded as 'INACTIVE'.
* Row Selection: Return all relevant columns from the data row to ensure full details (such as facility name, score, grade, and inspection date) are preserved for auditing.
* Columns Needed: Output all columns (*).

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: inspections
* Core Columns:
  - serial_number (Text): Unique identifier for the inspection event.
  - facility_name (Text): Name of the business establishment.
  - activity_date (Date): The date the inspection took place.
  - score (Big Int): Numeric score awarded during the inspection.
  - grade (Text): Letter grade assigned based on the score (e.g., 'A', 'B').
  - program_status (Text): Current administrative status of the program ('ACTIVE' or 'INACTIVE').
