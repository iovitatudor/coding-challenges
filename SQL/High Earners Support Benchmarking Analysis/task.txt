========================================================================
TASK NAME: High Earners Support Benchmarking Analysis
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify all high-earning staff members within the HR and Admin departments who earn a salary strictly greater than $80,000. Output their first name, last name, department, and salary.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Department Filter: Restrict rows to records where the department is either 'HR' or 'Admin'.
* Compensation Filter: Restrict rows to records where the salary is strictly greater than 80000.
* Operator Precedence: Ensure logical grouping is implemented so the salary constraint applies uniformly to both target departments.
* Columns Needed: first_name, last_name, department, salary

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: techcorp_workforce
* Core Columns:
  - first_name (Text) — The employee's first name.
  - last_name (Text) — The employee's last name.
  - department (Text) — The organizational department name.
  - salary (Big Int) — The employee's annual benchmarking salary.
