========================================================================
TASK NAME: Athletes Older Than 40 Winning Bronze or Silver
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find all athletes who were older than 40 years old when they won either
a Bronze or Silver medal.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: olympics_athletes_events
* Columns:
  - age (double precision)
  - medal (text)

FILTER CONDITIONS:
* age > 40
* medal is either Bronze or Silver (case-insensitive)

EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT:
* Select all relevant athlete/event columns matching the criteria.

========================================================================
