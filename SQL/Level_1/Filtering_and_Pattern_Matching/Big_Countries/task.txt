========================================================================
TASK NAME: Big Countries
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the name, population, and area of the big countries.
A country is big if it has an area of at least 3,000,000 sq km or
a population of at least 25,000,000.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: World
  - name (varchar)
  - continent (varchar)
  - area (int)
  - population (int)
  - gdp (bigint)
