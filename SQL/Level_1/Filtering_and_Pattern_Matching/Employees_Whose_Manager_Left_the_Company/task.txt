========================================================================
TASK NAME: Employees Whose Manager Left the Company
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the IDs of employees whose salary is strictly less than $30000 and
whose manager left the company (meaning their manager_id is not found in
the employee_id column of the Employees table). Return the result ordered
by employee_id.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - name (varchar)
  - manager_id (int)
  - salary (int)
