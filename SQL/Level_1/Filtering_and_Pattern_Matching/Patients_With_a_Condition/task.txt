========================================================================
TASK NAME: Patients With a Condition
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the patient_id, patient_name, and conditions of the patients who
have Type I Diabetes. Type I Diabetes always starts with a DIAB1 prefix
as a standalone condition code (at the start of the string or preceded by
a space).

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Patients
  - patient_id (int)
  - patient_name (varchar)
  - conditions (varchar)

========================================================================
