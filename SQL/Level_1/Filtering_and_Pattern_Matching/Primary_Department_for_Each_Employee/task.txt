========================================================================
TASK NAME: Primary Department for Each Employee
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report all employees with their primary department. For employees who belong
to only one department (where primary_flag is 'N'), report their only department.
For employees belonging to multiple departments, report the one where primary_flag is 'Y'.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employee
  - employee_id (int)
  - department_id (int)
  - primary_flag (enum: 'Y', 'N')

========================================================================
