========================================================================
TASK NAME: Article Views
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Retrieve the author_id, author_name, and publication_name for authors
whose articles got zero views. Sort the result by author_id in ascending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Views
  - article_id (int)
  - author_id (int)
  - author_name (varchar)
  - viewer_id (int)
  - view_count (int)
  - publication_name (varchar)

EXPECTED COLUMNS:
* author_id
* author_name
* publication_name
