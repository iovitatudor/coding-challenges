========================================================================
TASK NAME: Monthly Active Users January 2021
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Identify and calculate the total volume of Monthly Active Users (MAU) for the month of January 2021, broken down for each corporate account profile.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Time Constraint Filter: Restrict the evaluated records strictly to dates within January 2021 ('2021-01-01' to '2021-01-31' inclusive).
* Unique Aggregation: Count the distinct volume of user identifiers per account to eliminate multi-session activity bloat.
* Grouping Strategy: Segment the final aggregate calculation metrics uniformly by the account_id dimension.
* Columns Needed: account_id, monthly_count

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: sf_events
* Core Columns:
  - account_id (Varchar) — Unique corporate client account identifier.
  - user_id (Varchar) — Unique identifier tracking user logins or engagements.
  - record_date (Date) — The timestamp tracking when the activity occurred.
