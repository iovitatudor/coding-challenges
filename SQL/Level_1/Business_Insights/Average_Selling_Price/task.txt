========================================================================
TASK NAME: Average Selling Price
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the average selling price for each product rounded to 2 decimal places.
If a product does not have any sold units, its average selling price is 0.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Prices
  - product_id (int)
  - start_date (date)
  - end_date (date)
  - price (int)
* Table: UnitsSold
  - product_id (int)
  - purchase_date (date)
  - units (int)
