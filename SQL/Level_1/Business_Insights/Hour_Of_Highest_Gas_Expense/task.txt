========================================================================
TASK NAME: Hour Of Highest Gas Expense
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the hour of the single ride with the highest gasoline cost. Assume
only one ride has this maximum, so exactly one hour qualifies.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Table: lyft_rides
  - index (bigint)
  - weather (text)
  - hour (bigint)
  - travel_distance (double precision)
  - gasoline_cost (double precision)
* Output Format:
  - Print / select a single column "hour" for the record with max gasoline_cost.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input:
  index | weather | hour | travel_distance | gasoline_cost
  0     | cloudy  | 7    | 24.47           | 1.13
  1     | cloudy  | 23   | 23.67           | 1.99
  2     | sunny   | 17   | 20.93           | 0.86
* Output:
  hour: 23
  (Reason: 1.99 is the highest gasoline cost, occurring at hour 23.)

========================================================================
