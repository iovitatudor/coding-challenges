========================================================================
TASK NAME: December 2021 Pending Claims Volume Audit
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Count the total number of insurance or medical claims submitted during the month of December 2021 that are currently still pending resolution. A claim is considered pending if it does not have an official acceptance date or rejection date.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Time Filter: Limit records to submission dates between '2021-12-01' and '2021-12-31' inclusive.
* Status Filter: Isolate pending claims by ensuring both the acceptance date and rejection date fields are completely blank (NULL).
* Aggregation: Calculate a single total count of all matching records.
* Columns Needed: pending_claims_count

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: cvs_claims
* Core Columns:
  - claim_id (Big Int) — Unique identifier for each claim record.
  - date_submitted (Date) — The date the claim was initially filed.
  - date_accepted (Date) — The date the claim was approved (NULL if not approved).
  - date_rejected (Date) — The date the claim was denied (NULL if not denied).
