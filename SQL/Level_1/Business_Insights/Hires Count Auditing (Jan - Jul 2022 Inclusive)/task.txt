========================================================================
TASK NAME: Hires Count Auditing (Jan - Jul 2022 Inclusive)
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the total number of employees hired between the months of January and July in the year 2022 inclusive. The output must be a single aggregate count.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Time Filter: Restrict records to joining dates from '2022-01-01' through '2022-07-31'.
* Aggregation: Calculate a single total count of all matching employee rows.
* Columns Needed: employees_number

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: employees
* Core Columns:
  - id (Big Int) — Unique identification number for the employee.
  - joining_date (Date) — The official date the employee was hired.
