========================================================================
TASK NAME: Monthly Shipment Volume Tracking
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Calculate the total number of shipments processed per month. A single unique shipment is defined explicitly by a composite combination of the 'shipment_id' and 'sub_id' columns. Output the timeline group formatted as YYYY-MM alongside its corresponding shipment volume.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* String Truncation: Convert calendar shipment dates into standard text strings matching the 'YYYY-MM' format.
* Composite Uniqueness Counting: Combine and distinct-count the shipment_id and sub_id keys together to ensure complex package entries are aggregated accurately without row volume skewing.
* Grouping Strategy: Group data collections solely by the monthly timeline string to compile correct high-level monthly trends.
* Columns Needed: year_month, number_of_shipments

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: amazon_shipment
* Core Columns:
  - shipment_date (Date) — The date the shipment transaction occurred.
  - shipment_id (Big Int) — Primary tier tracking identification number.
  - sub_id (Big Int) — Secondary tier tracking sub-component number.
