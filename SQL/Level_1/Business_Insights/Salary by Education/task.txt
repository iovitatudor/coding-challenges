========================================================================
TASK NAME: Salary by Education
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Given a dataset containing the professional profiles and compensation metrics of a group of individuals, calculate the average annual salary associated with each distinct level of formal education.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Mathematical Aggregation: Use the mean/average calculation function to compute individual salary groups.
* Grouping: Segment the final aggregate calculation metrics uniformly by the formal education level dimension.
* Columns Needed: education, avg_salary

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: google_salaries
* Core Columns:
  - education (Text) — The highest level of academic completion achieved by the employee.
  - salary (Big Int) — The baseline annual compensation package amount.
