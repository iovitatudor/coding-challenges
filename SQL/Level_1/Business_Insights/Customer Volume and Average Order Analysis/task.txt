========================================================================
TASK NAME: Customer Volume and Average Order Analysis
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Audit order transactional history to determine both the total number of unique customers who successfully placed an order and the overall average financial value per order transaction.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Distinct Identification: Count individual customer profiles utilizing a distinct uniqueness mask to prevent multi-order users from inflating the customer volume tally.
* Financial Metrics: Calculate the arithmetic mean (AVG) of all order totals logged within the financial transaction column.
* Columns Needed: customers_qty, average_order_amount

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: postmates_orders
* Core Columns:
  - customer_id (Big Int) — Unique account tracking identifier for the client making the purchase.
  - amount (Double Precision) — The total financial value of the order transaction.
