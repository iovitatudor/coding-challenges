========================================================================
TASK NAME: List the Products Ordered in a Period
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Get the names of products that have at least 100 units ordered in February 2020
and their total amount.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Products
  - product_id (int)
  - product_name (varchar)
  - product_category (varchar)
* Table: Orders
  - product_id (int)
  - order_date (date)
  - unit (int)
