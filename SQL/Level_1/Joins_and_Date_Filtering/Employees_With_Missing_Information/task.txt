========================================================================
TASK NAME: Employees With Missing Information
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report the IDs of all employees with missing information (either name or
salary is missing across the Employees and Salaries tables).
Return the result ordered by employee_id in ascending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - name (varchar)
* Table: Salaries
  - employee_id (int)
  - salary (int)
