TASK NAME: Employee Bonus
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report the name and bonus amount of each employee who has a bonus
less than 1000 or did not receive any bonus.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employee
  - empId (int)
  - name (varchar)
  - supervisor (int)
  - salary (int)
* Table: Bonus
  - empId (int)
  - bonus (int)
