========================================================================
TASK NAME: The Number of Employees Which Report to Each Employee
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report the ids and names of all managers (employees who have at least 1 report),
the number of employees who report directly to them, and the average age of
their direct reports rounded to the nearest integer. Order by employee_id.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - name (varchar)
  - reports_to (int)
  - age (int)

========================================================================
