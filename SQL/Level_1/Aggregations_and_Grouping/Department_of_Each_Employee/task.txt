========================================================================
TASK NAME: Department of Each Employee
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Group employees by their department and display the total number of
employees in each department as total_employees.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - employee_name (varchar)
  - company (varchar)
  - category (varchar)
  - department (varchar)
  - salary (decimal)

EXPECTED COLUMNS:
* department
* total_employees

========================================================================
