========================================================================
TASK NAME: Departments With 5 Employees
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find all departments that have 5 or more employees.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: employee
* Relevant Columns:
  - department (text)
  - id (bigint)

AGGREGATION & FILTERING:
* GROUP BY department
* HAVING COUNT(id) >= 5 (or COUNT(*) >= 5)

EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT:
* department (text)
* number_of_employees (bigint)

========================================================================
