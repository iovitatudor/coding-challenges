========================================================================
TASK NAME: Bank Account Summary II
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Report the name and balance of users with a total balance higher than 10000.
The balance of an account is equal to the sum of the amounts of all transactions
involving that account.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Users
  - account (int)
  - name (varchar)
* Table: Transactions
  - trans_id (int)
  - account (int)
  - amount (int)
  - transacted_on (date)
