========================================================================
TASK NAME: Player Performance Insights
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the names and scores of the top 3 distinct players by highest score
who have won at least one match. Order output by score in descending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Players
  - player_id (int)
  - player_name (varchar)
  - score (int)
  - rank (int)

* Table: Matches
  - match_id (int)
  - player1 (varchar)
  - player2 (varchar)
  - winner (varchar)
  - match_date (date)

EXPECTED COLUMNS:
* player_name
* score
