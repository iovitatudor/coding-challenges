========================================================================
TASK NAME: Player Details
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Retrieve details of the last 5 matches played, including match_id, player_1,
player_2, winner, match_date, and the final score of the winning player.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Players
  - player_id (int)
  - player_name (varchar)
  - score (int)

* Table: Matches
  - match_id (int)
  - player_1 (varchar)
  - player_2 (varchar)
  - winner (varchar)
  - match_date (date)

EXPECTED COLUMNS:
* match_id
* player_1
* player_2
* winner
* match_date
* score
