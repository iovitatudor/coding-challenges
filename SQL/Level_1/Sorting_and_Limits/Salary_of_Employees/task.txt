========================================================================
TASK NAME: Salary of Employees
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Retrieve the employee_name, company, and salary for employees in the
'Full-Time' category, ordered by salary in descending order.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Table: Employees
  - employee_id (int)
  - employee_name (varchar)
  - company (varchar)
  - category (varchar)
  - department (varchar)
  - salary (decimal)

EXPECTED COLUMNS:
* employee_name
* company
* salary

========================================================================
