========================================================================
TASK NAME: Employees' Without Annual Review
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Return all employees who have never had an annual review. List the most recently hired employees first.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Exclusion Filtering: Isolate employees who do not exist in the annual review tracking log.
* Sorting Hierarchy: Sort the resulting dataset in descending order based on hire date.
* Columns Needed: first_name, last_name, hire_date, termination_date

TABLE SCHEMA:
* Table Name: uber_employees
  - id (Big Int) — Unique numerical identifier for the employee.
  - first_name (Text) — Employee's given name.
  - last_name (Text) — Employee's family name.
  - salary (Big Int) — Employee's current annual compensation.
  - hire_date (Date) — The calendar date the employee joined the company.
  - termination_date (Date) — The calendar date the employee left the company (if applicable).

* Table Name: uber_annual_review
  - id (Big Int) — Unique numerical identifier for the review log.
  - emp_id (Big Int) — Foreign key matching back to uber_employees(id).
  - review_date (Date) — The calendar date when the annual review occurred.
