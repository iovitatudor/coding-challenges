========================================================================
TASK NAME: Longest Word
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the longest word in a sentence. If two or more words share the maximum
length, retain and output the one that appears first in the sentence. Words are
separated by single spaces.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: A single line containing a text sentence (words separated by spaces).
* Output Format:
  - Print a single word representing the longest word in the sentence.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog
* Output: jumped
  (Reason: "jumped" has 6 letters, the most of any word in the sentence.)

* Input: red blue green cyan
* Output: green
  (Reason: "green" has 5 letters. "blue" and "cyan" have 4 letters.)
