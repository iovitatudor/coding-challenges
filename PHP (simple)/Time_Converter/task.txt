========================================================================
TASK NAME: Time Converter
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Convert a number of total seconds into hours, minutes, and seconds.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: An integer representing total seconds.
* Output Format:
  - [h]h [m]m [s]s followed by a newline.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: 3661
* Output: 1h 1m 1s

========================================================================
