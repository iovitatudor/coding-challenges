========================================================================
TASK NAME: Leap Year
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Determine if a given year is a leap year based on chronological
divisibility rules.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: Year (integer).
* Validation Rules:
  - Divisible by 4.
  - NOT divisible by 100, unless it is also divisible by 400.
* Output Format:
  - 'Leap year' or 'Not a leap year'

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: 2024
* Output: Leap year
