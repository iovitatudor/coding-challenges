========================================================================
TASK NAME: Smallest of Three
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Find the smallest of three numbers.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Three distinct sequential lines, each containing one number (integer).
* Output Format:
  - Smallest: [number]

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input:
  7
  2
  9
* Output:
  Smallest: 2
