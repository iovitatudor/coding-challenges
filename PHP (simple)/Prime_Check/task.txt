========================================================================
TASK NAME: Prime Check
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Determine whether a given integer (2 or greater) is a prime number.
A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that has no positive
divisors other than 1 and itself.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: An integer N (N >= 2).
* Output Format:
  - Print "Prime" if the number is prime, otherwise "Not prime".

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: 7
* Output: Prime
  (Reason: 7 is only divisible by 1 and 7.)

* Input: 9
* Output: Not prime
  (Reason: 9 is divisible by 3.)
