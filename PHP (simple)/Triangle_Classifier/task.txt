========================================================================
TASK NAME: Triangle Classifier
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Classify a triangle based on its side lengths.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Three distinct sequential lines, each containing a side length (integer).
* Validation Rules:
  - A triangle is valid if the sum of any two sides is strictly greater
    than the third side. Otherwise, output 'Not a triangle'.
* Classification Rules:
  - Equilateral: All three sides are equal.
  - Isosceles: Exactly two sides are equal.
  - Scalene: No sides are equal.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input:
  3
  4
  5
* Output: Scalene

========================================================================
