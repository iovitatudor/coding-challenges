========================================================================
TASK NAME: Sum of Numbers
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Read a list of numbers and calculate their sum.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - The first line is an integer indicating the total count of numbers to follow.
  - The subsequent lines contain exactly one number each.
* Calculation: Add all the subsequent numbers together.
* Output Format: Sum: [total]

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input:
  4
  10
  20
  30
  40
* Calculation: 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 = 100
* Output: Sum: 100
