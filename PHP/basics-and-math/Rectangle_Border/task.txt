========================================================================
TASK NAME: Rectangle Border
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Print a rectangle border made of stars based on input dimensions.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: Width of the rectangle (integer).
  - Line 2: Height of the rectangle (integer).
* Output Format:
  - The first and last rows are full rows of stars.
  - The rows in between have a star at the start and end, with spaces
    in the middle. No trailing whitespace characters.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input:
  5
  4
* Output:
  *****
  *   *
  *   *
  *****
