========================================================================
TASK NAME: Ticket Price
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Determine the ticket type and price based on age.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Age Categories:
  - Child (under 12 years old): $5
  - Adult (12 to 64 years old): $15
  - Senior (65 years old or more): $8
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: Age (integer).
* Output Format:
  - Line 1: [type]
  - Line 2: $[price]

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: 25
* Output:
  Adult
  $15
