========================================================================
TASK NAME: Reverse Number
========================================================================

TASK OVERVIEW:
Reverse the digits of a given positive integer and output the resulting
number with no leading zeros.

DATA REQUIREMENTS:
* Input Format:
  - Line 1: A positive integer N.
* Output Format:
  - Print the integer formed by reversing the digits of N.

EXAMPLES & EXPECTED OUTPUT:
* Input: 1234
* Output: 4321
  (Reason: Reversing 1234 yields 4321.)

* Input: 1200
* Output: 21
  (Reason: Reversing 1200 yields 0021, which becomes 21 without leading zeros.)
